Advancing the Future of Digital Risk

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) trains and deploys digital risk professionals through a unique model that combines academic collaboration with real-world industry practice.

About IDR

The Institute of Digital Risk is an industry-led training and deployment institute focused on preparing professionals for digital, cyber and technology risk roles in high-consequence and regulated environments.

Working in collaboration with leading academic institutions and industry partners, IDR bridges the gap between theory and practice by combining structured learning with real-world projects and practical deployment opportunities.

Our Model

Academy

Training programs and bootcamps designed for students and professionals to build expertise in digital risk, cyber security and AI governance.

Innovation & Incubation

Developing intellectual property, future risk frameworks and new approaches to AI governance and technology risk.

Advisory Services

Supporting organisations in implementing digital risk frameworks including NIST, ISO 27001 and NIS2 across complex operational environments.

Our Community

IDR serves a diverse community of students, early-career professionals and experienced practitioners seeking to develop expertise in digital, cyber and technology risk. The institute works closely with organisations in sectors such as financial services, critical infrastructure and technology to support workforce development and continuous upskilling.
